## 1. The Service

PlanSight is a web-based tool that allows you to upload project plan files (such as Microsoft Project `.mpp` files), visualise them as interactive task tables and Gantt charts, compute deterministic project insights, generate AI-powered analysis, and share read-only views with stakeholders.

PlanSight is **not** a project management tool. It does not edit, modify, or replace the source project plan. It is a visibility, analysis, and communication layer over plans you create elsewhere.

## 2. Account tiers

PlanSight offers three tiers:

- **Anonymous** — No account required. Upload, view, share, export, and access cached AI analysis. Plans uploaded anonymously are automatically deleted **24 hours** after upload.

- **Pro** — Paid subscription. Unlimited plans, regenerate AI analysis on demand, saved filtered views, annotations, PDF export, custom branding, higher upload limits, and other features described on our pricing page.

Specific tier limits (plan caps, file size caps, task count caps) are published on the pricing page and may change with notice.

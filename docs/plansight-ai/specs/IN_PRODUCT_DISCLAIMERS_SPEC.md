## Overview

Add in-product disclaimers at the points where users interact with computed Insights and AI Analysis output. Two distinct disclaimers for two distinct surfaces — they are not interchangeable.

1. **AI Analysis tab** — a persistent, info-styled banner stating that output is AI-generated, may contain errors, and requires human review before use in decisions.
2. **Insights tab** — a separate, lower-priority note about data quality (garbage-in-garbage-out), not an AI disclaimer because Insights are deterministic.
3. **Share view (stakeholder-facing page)** — both disclaimers appear, always-on, non-dismissible. Stakeholders are the audience most at risk of misreading output.
4. **Microcopy** — tooltip on the Regenerate button, "What is this?" link opening a fuller explanation modal.

The Terms of Service already disclaims AI Analysis accuracy in section 6. This spec implements the **in-product** version of that disclaimer, which is materially more protective than ToS-only coverage because it is visible *at the point of decision* rather than buried in a document accepted at signup.

---

## Why this matters

**Legal:** Australian Consumer Law and similar regimes weigh whether a disclaimer was "clear and conspicuous" at the point of decision, not just whether it existed in terms accepted at signup. A user who agreed to the ToS months ago cannot reasonably be expected to remember clause 6.2. A user who sees a disclaimer next to the AI output cannot reasonably claim they were misled. The in-product disclaimer is the one that holds up under scrutiny.

**Commercial:** The target buyer segment (PMs on serious projects) is *more* likely to trust the product when it acknowledges AI limitations openly. Absence of disclaimer is what worries serious buyers — it signals overclaiming. A calm, professional caption signals the vendor understands what the tool is and isn't.

**Practical:** Stakeholders viewing share links did not agree to the ToS. The in-product disclaimer is the *only* protection between them and a misread of AI output.

## Surface 1: AI Analysis tab — persistent banner

### Placement

A banner appears at the top of the AI Analysis tab content area, above the narrative summary, risks, and recommendations. Always visible on first view of the tab. Renders inside the tab content, not as a global toast or modal.

### Content

```
AI Analysis is generated by AI and may contain errors, omissions, or
outdated assessments. Review carefully and apply your own judgement
before sharing with stakeholders or making project decisions. Not a
substitute for professional project management expertise.
```

Three sentences. Plain language. Action-oriented ("review carefully", "apply your own judgement"). Explicit that this isn't replacing the PM's expertise.

### Visual style

- Info-styled, not warning-styled. Soft background colour (use the `info` or `muted` design token from `BRANDKIT.md`, not the `warning` or `destructive` token).
- Info icon (Lucide `Info` or `AlertCircle` with info colouring) on the left.
- Body text at the body-text size, not smaller. Disclaimers in small grey text get ignored — they should be readable at a glance.
- Full width of the tab content area. Bordered or background-tinted card.
- Vertical spacing: ~16px above the first AI Analysis section beneath it.

### Dismissibility

- **Signed-in PMs:** Banner has a small "Dismiss" or "×" control. Once dismissed, it stays dismissed for the rest of that browser session. State is stored client-side only (sessionStorage or React state — not persisted across sessions or devices). On the next session, it appears again on first view of the tab.
- **Anonymous users:** Banner is dismissible per session, same as signed-in PMs.
- **Share view (stakeholders):** Banner is **not dismissible**. No close control rendered. See Surface 3.

The dismiss-per-session model balances "don't annoy power users who already know" against "reset the trust contract each session." A user opening the app on Monday morning sees the disclaimer once and then has a clean view for the day.

### Acceptance criteria

1. Banner renders on every first view of the AI Analysis tab in a new session.
2. Banner can be dismissed by signed-in and anonymous users; dismissal persists for the current session only.
3. Banner re-appears on the next session.
4. Banner is visually prominent (body-text size, not grey footnote) but not alarming (info-styled, not warning-styled).
5. Banner is always visible on share view pages, with no dismiss control rendered.

## Surface 2: Insights tab — data-quality note

### Why this is different

The Insights tab content is **not AI-generated.** It's deterministic — the PMP-aligned CPM engine computes critical path, total float, RAG status, and similar values from the task data in the plan. Same inputs always produce the same outputs.

Putting an AI disclaimer here would be **misleading** (it would suggest the deterministic math is unreliable, undermining your real differentiator). But a *different* note is worth adding — one that addresses the actual limitation: the math is only as good as the input data.

### Placement

Below the page header for the Insights tab, above the first insight panel (RAG status, critical path, etc.). Smaller than the AI Analysis banner — this is a clarifying note, not a disclaimer with legal weight.

### Content

```
Insights are calculated from the task data in your uploaded plan.
Accuracy depends on the quality and completeness of that data —
missing dependencies, incorrect durations, or unset baselines will
produce misleading results. Review the underlying tasks if any
insight looks unexpected.
```

This sets honest expectations (the insights *are* reliable given good inputs, but garbage-in-garbage-out is real) and gives the user something actionable (go check the source data).

### Visual style

- Smaller than the AI banner — single line if possible, two lines on mobile.
- Use the `muted` or `secondary` text colour from `BRANDKIT.md`. This is informational, not protective.
- Info icon optional. Could use a small text-only italic note. Designer's call.
- No dismiss control. Always present, low visual weight.

### Acceptance criteria

1. Note renders at the top of the Insights tab on every view.
2. Note is visually de-emphasised (muted colour, smaller than the AI banner).
3. Note does **not** use language suggesting Insights are AI-generated or unreliable. It frames the limit as data quality, not engine quality.
4. Note appears on share view pages as well as the PM's own view.

## Surface 3: Share view — both disclaimers, always-on

The public share page (stakeholder-facing) is the most important place to get this right. Stakeholders did not agree to the ToS. They may not know AI was involved. They may treat the output as PM-endorsed analysis.

### What appears

- The **AI Analysis disclaimer banner** at the top of any section displaying AI Analysis, with **no dismiss control**.
- The **Insights data-quality note** at the top of the Insights section, identical to the PM-facing version.

### What does *not* appear on the share view

- Dismiss controls on the AI banner. Stakeholders see it every page load. There is no per-session memory of dismissal for share viewers, because share viewers may not be returning users at all.
- "Regenerate" buttons or any PM-only controls. The share view is read-only.

### Slight rewording for the share view (optional)

The default banner content works on share view too, but you may consider a slight tonal shift to address the audience directly:

```
This analysis was generated by AI and may contain errors, omissions,
or outdated assessments. Treat it as one input among several — not
as a final verdict — and check with your project contact before
relying on it for decisions.
```

The change: from "review carefully and apply your own judgement" (PM voice) to "treat it as one input among several... check with your project contact" (stakeholder voice). Either version is acceptable. The PM-voice version is fine as a starting point; the stakeholder-voice version is a polish item.

### Acceptance criteria

1. AI Analysis banner is always visible on share view pages with no dismiss control.
2. Insights data-quality note is always visible on share view pages.
3. No PM-only controls (Regenerate, edit, settings) render on the share view.

## Surface 4: Microcopy and supporting elements

### Regenerate button tooltip (Pro feature)

When a Pro user hovers the Regenerate button on the AI Analysis tab:

```
Re-run Claude analysis on the current plan. Results may differ
between runs.
```

This pre-empts the "why is the output different now?" support ticket and reinforces the non-deterministic nature of AI output. Single sentence, tooltip-appropriate.

### Pro upgrade prompt for Regenerate (Free tier)

When a Free tier user clicks Regenerate, the upgrade modal opens. Copy:

```
Regenerate AI Analysis any time as your plan evolves.
A Pro feature.
```

Implicitly signals that AI Analysis ages and benefits from refreshing — which itself reinforces "this is a snapshot in time, not a permanent verdict."

### "What is this?" link

Near the AI Analysis tab header (small text, secondary colour), add a link or icon button labelled **"What is this?"** or **"About AI Analysis"**. Opens a modal with the fuller explanation:

```
AI Analysis is generated by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, based on
the structured task data in your uploaded plan. It produces three things:

1. A narrative summary of the plan's current state
2. Identified risks, referencing specific tasks
3. Prescriptive recommendations the PM can consider

What it does NOT do:
• Replace human project management expertise or professional judgement
• Guarantee accuracy — AI can make errors, miss context, or produce
  outdated assessments
• Constitute project management, financial, legal, or other professional
  advice
• Train on your data — Anthropic does not use API inputs to train models

How to use it well:
• Treat AI Analysis as a starting point, not a final answer
• Cross-check identified risks against your own knowledge of the project
• Use recommendations as prompts for your own thinking, not as instructions
• Re-run analysis after meaningful plan changes (Pro feature)

For full details, see our Terms of Service section 6.
```

Modal includes a single "Got it" close button. No dismiss-permanently option — the link should always be available for the curious or for stakeholders.

### Acceptance criteria

1. Regenerate button tooltip appears on hover with the specified text.
2. Pro upgrade modal for Regenerate includes the specified copy.
3. "What is this?" link is visible near the AI Analysis tab header on both PM-facing and share view pages.
4. Clicking the link opens a modal with the full explanation and a single close action.

## Critical do-nots

These mistakes will undermine the value of the work. Do not:

### 1. Use warning-styled visuals

Do **not** use red, orange, or yellow alert-style banners with warning icons. "WARNING: AI may be wrong" feels accusatory and trains users to dismiss similar UI patterns elsewhere. Neutral, professional info styling lands legally and commercially. Tone matters as much as content.

### 2. Require checkbox acknowledgement to view AI Analysis

Do **not** gate AI Analysis behind "I understand this is AI-generated" checkboxes. This trains users to click through without reading and adds friction without legal benefit. The persistent banner achieves the same protective goal without the friction.

### 3. Conflate Insights with AI

Do **not** apply the AI Analysis disclaimer language to the Insights tab. Insights are deterministic CPM math, not AI output. Disclaiming them as AI would undersell your real differentiator (PMP-aligned, defensible calculations) and overclaim about the AI side of the product. Two distinct disclaimers for two distinct surfaces.

### 4. Use small grey footnote text for the AI banner

The AI Analysis disclaimer must be readable at a glance. Small grey text below the AI output gets ignored and provides weaker legal protection than a prominent info banner above the output. Body-text size, info-styled card. The Insights note can be quieter; the AI banner cannot.

### 5. Make the share view disclaimer dismissible

Share view stakeholders are the highest-risk audience and the lowest-context audience. The disclaimer must be unmissable on every page load, no dismiss control rendered. Do not add per-session memory for share viewers.

Components to add or modify:

Storage:

- Dismissal state for the AI banner uses `sessionStorage` keyed by something like `ai-disclaimer-dismissed`. Cleared on session end. Not persisted per-user.

Data model:

- No database changes required.

API routes:

- No new API routes required.

## Acceptance criteria — overall feature

The feature is shippable when:

1. The AI Analysis tab renders the disclaimer banner at the top, body-text size, info-styled.
2. The banner is dismissible for signed-in and anonymous users; dismissal persists for the session only.
3. The banner re-appears on the next session.
4. The Insights tab renders the data-quality note at the top, muted styling, no dismiss control.
5. The Insights note does not use AI-disclaimer language.
6. The share view page renders both the AI banner (non-dismissible) and the Insights note.
7. The Regenerate button shows the specified tooltip on hover.
8. The Pro upgrade modal for Regenerate shows the specified copy.
9. The "What is this?" link is present near the AI Analysis tab header on both PM-facing and share view pages, opening the explanation modal.
10. No surface uses warning-styled visuals, checkbox-gates AI Analysis, or applies AI language to the Insights tab.
